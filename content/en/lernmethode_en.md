# Lernmethode

Lernplanung told you **when** to study. Lernquellen told you **what** to study with. This page tells you **how** to study so that it sticks.

Every skill has its own method. If you ignore the method, you will put in the hours and still come up short in the exam.

---

# Lehrbuch and Arbeitsbuch

This is the backbone of everything. Work through every lesson in **two rounds**: the first to get familiar, the second to master it.

## Round one — getting familiar

1. **Watch the video lesson**
2. **Read the whole lesson once** in the Lehrbuch

The goal of this round is not understanding, only familiarity. If you don't understand something, move past it.

## Round two — mastery

1. **Watch the video** again
2. **Vocabulary** of the lesson
3. **Memorization items** of the lesson
4. **Grammar** in the Lehrbuch
5. **The texts**
6. **Writing exercises**
7. **The audio**
8. **Speaking exercises**
9. **The Arbeitsbuch exercises**
10. **Grammatik Aktiv**

> **Memorization items in every lesson means:** Redemittel · separable verbs · verbs with dative and accusative · irregular verbs · Verben mit Präpositionen

## Rules for working with the textbook

- **One lesson every 8 hours.** If you study 4 hours a day, a lesson takes two days.
- **Translate the texts.**
- **Review the lesson the next day** before moving on to the next one.
- **Solve everything first without looking at the answers.** Compare afterwards.
- **Correct in a different colour** so you can see your mistakes when you review.
- **Do exactly what the book asks** — nothing else.

# 1. Grammatik — Grammar

> **Grammar without practice has exactly zero effect.**

- **Sentence first, rule second.** Look at how the rule appears in the sentences of the lesson, then go to the rule itself.
- **Practise immediately after learning.** A gap between learning and practising undoes all of it.
- **Say the rule from memory**, then check it against the book.
- **Build your own sentence with every new rule** — about your own life, not the one in the book.
- **Grammatik Aktiv** comes after the Arbeitsbuch exercises, to consolidate.
- **Spaced review:** revisit each rule after one day, one week and one month.

---

# 2. Wortschatz — Vocabulary

- **The Leitner box.** What you know comes back later, what you don't comes back sooner.
- **Build the card with a sentence, not a single word.** A lone word disappears quickly.
- **Always with the article and the plural.** Don't learn `Tisch` — learn `der Tisch, die Tische`.
- **A picture instead of a translation.** Wherever possible, put an image on the back rather than a translation.
- **Learn words inside a network of words**, not one by one. Next to `der Arzt` put: `die Ärztin`, `die Praxis`, `der Termin`, `krank`, `untersuchen`.
- **Use every new word in a sentence of your own the same day.**

> **Coming soon:** the **VOX** app will do all of this — image, sentence, article, word network and Leitner system. It is still being built.

---

# 3. Auswendiglernen — Memorization

- **Memorise whole phrases, not single words.** Learn `Ich interessiere mich für...`, not `interessieren`.
- **Repeat out loud.** Memorisation does not work with your eyes.
- **Write it from memory**, then compare with the original.
- **Use it the same week** in your writing and speaking. Whatever you don't use, you lose.

---

# 4. Lesen — Reading

- **Read the text several times**, not once and not twice.
- **First round rough, the following ones precise.** First just work out what it is about.
- **First round without a dictionary.** Guess unknown words from context.
- **Underline unknown words** and look them all up at the end.
- **Translate the text.**
- **Read out loud.** It trains your pronunciation and the text sticks better.
- **Finally, summarise the text in your own words** — without looking at it.

---

# 5. Schreiben — Writing

- **Use the model text in the book as a template.** Keep the structure, change the topic.
- **Keep Redemittel ready.** A few fixed phrases for each text type (email, complaint, invitation).
- **Write ← get it corrected ← write it again.** That rewrite is the most important part. A text that was corrected but never rewritten is only half an exercise.
- **Keep an error notebook.** Every mistake your partner or teacher finds goes into a separate notebook.
- **Sometimes write under time pressure** so you are ready for the exam.

---

# 6. Sprechen — Speaking

- **Find a language partner.** This is the most serious advice on this page — speaking is the one skill that cannot be completed alone.
- **Shadowing:** speak along with the audio at the same time, at the same speed and with the same intonation.
- **Record your voice and listen back.** You will hear your own mistakes.
- **Talk in front of a mirror.**
- **Substitution method:** take the dialogue from the book and swap the words marked in green (the replaceable ones) for your own details.
- **Talk to yourself.** Describe whatever you see in German on your way to work.
- **Prepare one topic per week** and speak about it for two minutes.

---

# 7. Hören — Listening

**The main method — listening while writing:**

1. Start the audio and **write while you listen**
2. Listen again and **speak along with it**
3. Write again and fill in what you missed
4. Keep going until you are **certain you cannot get anything more out of that audio**

A single audio can take **a week**. That is normal.

> **Only once you can speak like the audio itself are you allowed to look at the transcript.** Not before.

**Supporting methods:**

- **Shadowing** — speak along with the audio
- **Passive listening** — play the same audio while cooking, exercising or on the metro
- **Change the speed** — 0.8× first, 1.2× later

# Evidence-based methods

These are general learning methods with research behind them, and they work across all seven skills.

### Retrieval practice
Test yourself **before** you reread. Try to pull the material out of your memory without looking. No method has stronger research behind it.

### The Feynman technique
Explain a grammar rule in simple words to someone else. Wherever you get stuck is exactly the part you don't know yet.

### Lexical chunks
Language is made of phrases, not single words. This is precisely the scientific basis for what you are already doing with Redemittel.

### Reviewing before sleep
The brain consolidates memory during sleep. Ten minutes in the evening are worth more than thirty at midday.

### The error notebook — Fehlerheft
Write every mistake your partner or teacher finds into a separate notebook. Go through it once a month. The recurring mistakes are the map of your weaknesses.

### Dictogloss
Listen to a short text several times, then rewrite it in your own words. Very effective from B1 upwards.

# ‼️ Don't do strange things

This is where most of the time gets wasted:

- **Don't read the transcript instead of listening.** People who do this fail the Hören part of the exam.
- **Don't read and translate the exercise questions instead of solving them** and then move on.
- **Don't look at the answers before you have tried.**
- **Do exactly what the book asks of you.**

If you have any question at all — grammar, vocabulary, anything — ask in the group. Everything from A1 to C2 gets answered.
