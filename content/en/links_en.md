# Wichtige Links

Every source named on the previous three pages has its address here — organised by the same seven skills.

We are a team of ten, and we have accompanied a lot of learners from zero all the way to a passed B2 exam, free of charge. Every channel and group listed here is free, and the teachers who answer there do it voluntarily.

> ℹ️ **A note on language:** most of these channels are aimed at Persian-speaking learners. You can of course still use all the German material — the audio, films, music and texts. Channels whose explanations are in Persian are marked as such.

Lernplanung told you to share your routine with the others. The place for that is a **separate topic inside this group**:

1. Join the questions and answers group first
2. Then open the **Fortschritt teilen** topic inside the group
3. Send the result from **Konto ← Fortschritt teilen** there

The link to that topic will not work for you until you have joined the group.
