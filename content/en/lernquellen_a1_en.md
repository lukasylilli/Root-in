# Lernquellen — A1

In Lernplanung you saw that a language is built from seven skills. This page tells you **what to use** for each of those seven skills at A1 level.

Links to every source named here are on the **Wichtige Links** page.

## The minimum you need

If the number of names here feels overwhelming, get just these six things and start:

1. **Lehrbuch + Arbeitsbuch** — Pluspunkt Deutsch: Leben in Deutschland A1
2. **Grammatik Aktiv A1–B1**
3. **A flashcard app** — Anki, or a paper Leitner box
4. **The VIP files from LukasAlmani** (Telegram)
5. **Pimsleur audio**
6. **A language partner**

The rest of this page explains where each of them fits.

## Overview: which source for which skill

| Skill | Main source |
|---|---|
| Grammatik | Grammatik Aktiv + textbook + YouTube LukasAlmani |
| Wortschatz | Words from your textbook + flashcard app |
| Auswendiglernen | VIP files from LukasAlmani |
| Lesen | Texts in the Lehrbuch |
| Schreiben | Textbook exercises + letters to your language partner |
| Sprechen | Language partner + shadowing + voice recordings |

## Which textbook should I buy?

Any established course book works. All of these are fine:

- Pluspunkt Deutsch: Leben in Deutschland A1
- Menschen A1
- Schritte International Neu A1
- Sicher! A1
- Starten wir! A1
- Spektrum Deutsch A1
- Studio [21] A1

**Which one is yours?** Three criteria:

1. The book you can find lessons and videos for
2. The book you learn from easily
3. The book your course tells you to buy

**Two rules:**

- Whichever book you pick, **do not switch it before the end of B1**. From B1+ onwards we recommend Aspekte neu.
- Wherever this app says "textbook", it means one of these books. We recommend **Pluspunkt**, and the pages in this app are written around it.

**Note that the course book comes in two parts.** Buy both:

- **Lehrbuch** — the lessons
- **Arbeitsbuch** — the exercises

## 1. Grammatik — Grammar

- **Grammatik Aktiv A1–B1** — your main grammar book
- **The grammar at the end of each Lehrbuch lesson** — it is usually summarised there
- **The LukasAlmani YouTube channel** — if you study on your own, learn each lesson's grammar here
- **The grammar exercises in the Arbeitsbuch** — always solve them after learning

At A1 your focus is: sein and haben, building simple sentences, der/die/das, plural forms, and questions with wo / wie / was.

## 2. Wortschatz — Vocabulary

**New words:** only the words from the lesson you have just studied. They are usually listed at the end of each lesson in the Arbeitsbuch.

**Daily review (base layer):** a flashcard app using the Leitner system. For now, one of these:

- **Anki**
- a paper **Leitner box**

> **Coming soon:** we are building **VOX**, an app made specifically for vocabulary, which will replace all of these.

## 3. Auswendiglernen — Memorization

**The VIP files from LukasAlmani** on Telegram. This is exactly the material you simply have to know by heart:

- Redemittel
- verbs with dative and accusative
- separable verbs
- irregular verbs
- Verben mit Präpositionen

**Tip:** print them out and keep them next to you.

> **Coming soon:** this will move into the VOX app as well.

## 4. Lesen — Reading

- **The texts in the Lehrbuch** — only from the lesson you are working on
- **The reading comprehension exercises in the Arbeitsbuch** — answer the short questions about the text
- **The longer texts in the book** — review them once a week

## 5. Schreiben — Writing

- **The writing exercises from the same lesson in your book**
- **An email to your language partner** — a new topic every time
- **A short text or dialogue** about the topic of your new lesson

**Important:** send what you wrote to your language partner so they can correct it, and correct theirs in return. Writing without correction is only half the exercise.

At A1, start with these: introducing yourself (name, age, job, family) and short everyday messages, such as arranging to meet or asking how someone is.

## 6. Sprechen — Speaking

- **A language partner** — for now, find one in the Telegram group *(coming soon in the VOX app)*
- **The dialogue and Redemittel exercises in your textbook**
- **Shadowing** — speak along with the audio at the same time
- **Talk in front of a mirror and record your voice**

Talk to your partner about all sorts of topics and introduce yourself fully: family, interests, work, daily routine.

## 7. Hören — Listening

This skill has two separate places:

**Base layer (10 minutes, every day):**
The **Pimsleur audio**. Listen and repeat. That's all.

**Plan block (25 minutes, on free days):**
The audio from the lesson you are working on. The method: listen until you know it, write it down **without looking at the transcript**, then compare what you wrote with the original.

**Extra sources:**

- **Nicos Weg** (from DW)
- **podcasts for A1 learners**
- simple **audiobooks**
- the **Readle** app

## An example weekly plan

This is the plan I used to pass my own A1. You do not have to follow it exactly — adapt it to yourself.

| Day | Programme |
|---|---|
| **Friday** | VIP files (memorization) · speaking in front of the mirror + recording · talking with your language partner · audiobook · Nicos Weg · reviewing longer texts from the book |
| **Saturday** | Pimsleur audio · Lehrbuch and Arbeitsbuch |
| **Sunday** | Pimsleur audio · daily words · Grammatik Aktiv |
| **Monday** | A1 words · Lehrbuch and Arbeitsbuch |
| **Tuesday** | Words from the textbook · writing an email and a text or dialogue · exchanging corrections with your partner |
| **Wednesday** | Daily words (Lilli) · Lehrbuch and Arbeitsbuch · Nicos Weg |
| **Thursday** | Podcast · daily words (Lilli) · Lehrbuch and Arbeitsbuch · Readle |

## A few notes

- **If you have already worked through a book, don't do it again.** Spend that time on your textbook instead.
- **The textbook matters most.** Work through it until you know it by heart — the texts, the audio and the grammar.
- **Practise all seven skills together.** A good plan leaves none of them out.
- **The LukasAlmani Telegram and YouTube channels** are full of free files and study notes. The addresses are on the Wichtige Links page.
